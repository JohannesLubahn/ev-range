# About this website

This website was developed to help people understand the capabilities and limits of BEV cars. It has no affiliation, no advertisement and no sponsors. I develop and maintain this tool in my spare time. I try to be accurate when setting formulas and values for car models, but mistakes happen and not all of them can be corrected.
