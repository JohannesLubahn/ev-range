# About this website

This website helps people understand what battery-electric cars (BEVs) can and cannot do. It started as a personal helper for my own decision to buy an EV in the Thai market. AI helped me refine my calculations, turn my nerdy spreadsheet into a (still nerdy) website, and add more markets and car models.

I have only a basic understanding of physics, mechanics and electric power, no knowledge of batteries, and no special expertise in any of the related fields. I try to be accurate when setting formulas and values for the car models, but mistakes happen, and not all of them can be corrected.

I hope this tool helps other people as much as it helped me with my purchase. I publish it for the greater good: the website has no advertisement, no sponsors and no affiliations, and it collects no data about how you use it. All your inputs stay in your browser until you clear its site data. I intend to keep it this way indefinitely.

## Accuracy and feedback

The current model is expected to be within class 1 accuracy (−10 % to +15 %). If a result does not match what you experience with your car, you can send me your data at [johannes.lubahn@gmail.com](mailto:johannes.lubahn@gmail.com?subject=EV%20Range) with the subject line "EV Range". Please note that some cars deviate from their production specifications, and that your experience may come from an effect the model does not cover. I maintain this project in my spare time and with spare tokens, so I may answer very late or not at all.

## For manufacturers

If you find your cars treated unfairly, please contact me politely and we can look into it. Please note that the models and specifications prefer real-world tests over marketing claims and lab results.
