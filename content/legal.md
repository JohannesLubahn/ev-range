## Estimates, not advice

All figures on this site are estimates from a model and from published data. They are not measurements of your car and not advice on buying, driving or charging one. Real range, consumption, battery life and costs depend on the car, the weather, the road and how it is driven. I check the data with care, but it may be wrong, incomplete or out of date. ## Privacy

- The site sets no cookies and has no analytics, tracking or advertising. It loads nothing from other servers when you open it.
- **Route lookup (only when you press "Get route" on the start page):** your browser then sends the two places you typed to [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org/) (OpenStreetMap Foundation) to find them, the two coordinates to the routing server of [FOSSGIS e. V.](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/) for the road distance, and points along the route to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) for the heights (Copernicus DEM). Like any web request, this passes your IP address to these services; see their privacy statements. Nothing is sent before you press the button, and the places are not stored by this site beyond your own browser.
- Your car selection, scenarios and settings (also those of the start page) are saved only in your own browser and are not sent to anyone, apart from the route lookup above. To delete them, clear this site's data in your browser.
- A share link carries your settings in the link itself, so whoever receives it can see them.
- The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, it records technical data such as your IP address when you open a page, to deliver the site and keep it secure. I have no access to these records; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply and do not pass them on.
- Questions about your data: write to the address above.
